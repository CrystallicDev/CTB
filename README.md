## Description
**CTB** backports content from the **Pale Garden Awakens** and **Tricky Trials** updates to older Minecraft versions.

Among other things :
* The **Creaking** and its **Creaking Heart**, with the full pale oak wood set, pale moss, hanging moss, eyeblossoms and resin
* The **Breeze**, its **Wind Charges** and the trial mob effects (Infested, Oozing, Weaving, Wind Charged, Trial Omen...)
* The **copper family** blocks (bulb, grate, door, trapdoor) and their weathering
* The **Cherry Grove** biome and its wood set

Creatures are rendered with [GeckoLib](https://github.com/bernie-g/geckolib), with models, textures and animations remade from scratch.
